Privacy policy

Effective October 5, 2026

Short version: the Fuck Corpo app keeps everything on your phone. We don't run accounts, servers, ads, or analytics for the app, so there's nothing for us to collect, sell, or leak.

What the app stores

  • Your pay details (pay type, wage, hours per week, currency) and the boss name you enter.
  • Your break sessions (type, start time, duration, amount earned).
  • App settings, such as your receipt style and whether Pro is unlocked.

All of this is stored locally on your device. It is never sent to us or to any third party by the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Sharing

When you tap Share or Export, the app creates an image or CSV file and hands it to your phone's share sheet. Where it goes next is up to you and the app you pick (for example Instagram or Messages), and that app's privacy policy applies.

Purchases

If you buy Pro, the payment is handled by Google Play. We don't receive your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to that transaction.

This website

The website has no accounts, cookies, ads, or analytics. The calculator runs entirely in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere. Our hosting provider, Vercel, processes standard technical data (such as IP address and browser type) to serve the site and keep it secure.

Children

The app contains crude humor and strong language and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.

A contact email will be listed here before the app launches on Google Play.
